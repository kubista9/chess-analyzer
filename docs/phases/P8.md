## What was done

### Scheduler (`shared/training/scheduler.ts`, pure)
- One Leitner scheduler, with parameters per kind:
  - line cards: 1, 3, 7, 16, 35, 60 days;
  - mistake cards: 2, 5, 14, 30, 90 days. A mistake card retires after 3 correct answers in a row, once its interval reaches 21 days.
- Box 0 means new. A card is introduced into box 1, due now.
- A correct first try in box b schedules the card `boxesDays[b-1]` days ahead and moves it up one box. The fuzz is ±5%, derived deterministically from an FNV hash of the id and the box.
- A wrong answer sends the card to box 1, due tomorrow, with one more lapse.
- Session order is (1 + overdue intervals) × weight.
- New cards are capped at 5 per kind per day, counted from local midnight.
- `resetForRecurrence` makes a card due now, with one more lapse, and un-retires it.

### Cards (`shared/training/cards.ts`, pure)
- **Repertoire graph.** A breadth-first walk from the start, to ply 16:
  - at the owner's nodes it follows the entry's move;
  - at the opponent's nodes it follows every legal reply that lands on an entry.
  - So no reply is ever played without a prepared answer, and transpositions share one node.
- **Line cards.** One per reachable owner-to-move entry:
  - `primary` is the repertoire move;
  - the weight is the tree's wN at the node (at least 0.5);
  - the name is the deepest book name of the line, including the move.
- **Mistake cards.** One per (colour, EPD) where a game's first owner error happened. That is the first owner move with a loss ≥ 5 (inaccuracy or worse) in the first 20 plies, taken only when every earlier owner move is scored.
  - `sources` lists each game (id, date, opponent, rating, ply, played move, loss), newest first.
  - The weight is the sum of the games' recency weights (H = 90).
- **Dedupe.** A mistake site that is also a line node becomes ONE line card, with the games attached as `sources`. The UI shows "You went wrong here in N games" as a muted note inside the blue card, never in the mistake styling. No (colour, EPD) has two active cards.
- **`mergeCards`** (regeneration) is idempotent: the same drafts give no writes.
  - The SRS state and the review history stay with the card id.
  - A card that is no longer generated is marked `removed`, and restored with its state if it comes back.
  - A source game newer than the card's last review brings the card back, due now with one more lapse. This also applies to a retired card, and to a line card carrying mistake games.
  - A deep-check rejection is reopened if a new game repeats a move there.

### Line runs (`shared/training/lineDrill.ts`)
- **`sampleLineRun`** picks each opponent reply by its tree edge's weighted count. It uses only the replies that have an entry.
  - Without game data it falls back to the book's first reply, else to a uniform choice.
  - Replies whose subtree holds a card still to grade get an 8× boost.
  - The RNG is seeded (mulberry32).
  - A run stops at the repertoire's leaf or at ply 16.
- **`planLineRuns`** samples runs until every due card of the colour is covered, up to 6 runs. A card is graded once per session.

### Judging (`shared/training/judge.ts`)
- **Line cards:** only the repertoire move is `correct`. Another move with a loss below 5 is `sound-other`: neutral, with no lapse and no promotion (the critic's rule). Anything else, including a move the engine cannot score, is `wrong`.
- **Mistake cards:**
  - `best`: a loss below 1;
  - `good-enough`: a loss below 3 (the Retry tolerance, `judgeRetry`'s thresholds);
  - `repertoire`: the repertoire move with a loss below 5;
  - otherwise `wrong`.
- `acceptableMoves` lists the accepted set from the scored moves at the root.
- Only the first non-neutral try in a presentation changes the schedule.

### Store and server (`server/db/migrations.ts` v5, `server/db/drills.ts`, `server/services/drillService.ts`, `server/routes.ts`)
- **Tables.**
  - `drill_cards`: primary key (username, id) and unique (username, kind, color, epd). It holds the content JSON, the confirm state, the deep check JSON and the Leitner columns.
  - `drill_reviews`: an append-only log of every answer, with its verdict, loss, graded flag, attempts, ms, box before/after and the due time after.
  - `drill_meta`: the regeneration stamp.
- **Lazy regeneration.** The stamp is the games stamp, the owner-tier cache generation, the repertoire stamp and today's date. When it changes, the next drills request rebuilds the cards over the 6-month window (H = 90) and merges them. So sync, backfill (including `npm run backfill` in another process) and repertoire edits all regenerate the cards.
  - The sync job also regenerates explicitly.
  - Repertoire PUT, DELETE and seed-apply refresh the cards in the background.
- **New cards.**
  - **Line cards** are introduced in the order: the owner's own fresh edits first, then weight descending, then ply. Entries the owner edited in the last 24 h (`source = 'edited'`: Set as my move, Adopt) skip the cap, so an adopted alternative drills at once. A seed does not skip it.
  - **Mistake cards** go through the lazy deep check (the critic's correction):
    - Only the day's new-slot candidates are checked, with up to 2 extra per slot for rejections.
    - A cached deep row that scores the games' moves settles a card at once.
    - Otherwise one background job, `drills:check` (type `drill-check`, interactive priority), runs the deep searches. `played` holds the source moves, so they are scored depth-matched.
    - A card is confirmed when the deep loss of its worst source move is ≥ 5, else rejected (hidden). Confirmed cards are introduced while slots remain.
    - `GET /drills/stats` never starts the job (Home and the nav badge must not start Stockfish). A lines-only session does not start it either.
- **Endpoints.**
  - `GET /api/drills/stats`.
  - `GET /api/drills/due?kind=&focus=<id>|color=&moves=|epd=`: the session. It returns mixed items (one line run, then two positions, and so on), the stats, the check job and a `focusNote`.
  - `POST /api/drills/answer {id, uci, ms, attempts}`. It validates legality (400) and answers 404 for a removed card.
    - Line cards: soundness comes from the deep row if it already scores the move, else from an owner-tier searchmoves follow-up.
    - Mistake cards: the move is judged against the deep best, and a move not in the row gets a depth-matched deep searchmoves.
    - The response carries `{verdict, correct, graded, loss, repertoire, acceptable[], best, played (SAN lines), box, nextDue, retired, searched}`.
  - `POST /api/drills/regenerate`.

### Verify (`scripts/verify/verify-cards.ts`, read-only)
- It generates the cards in memory from the store. It uses the stored repertoire, or an in-memory seed when that is empty (it is empty in the owner's store).
- It prints the counts by kind and colour, and today's due list (the stored state, plus the new cards the caps would let in).
- It checks:
  - one card per (colour, EPD) and the id format;
  - that every mistake card has sources at its ply with a loss ≥ 5;
  - that line cards match their entries;
  - idempotent regeneration and determinism;
  - the 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 acceptance.

## Deviations from the spec, and why

- **SQLite, not training.json / drill-log.jsonl** (OVERRIDES). The tables are `drill_cards`, `drill_reviews` and `drill_meta`.
- **Kinds are `repertoire-line` and `own-mistake`**, following the spec. The badge text is "POSITION FROM YOUR GAME", which is the brief's wording; the spec's was "FROM YOUR GAME".
- **The mistake source is the first error per game, with loss ≥ 5** (the brief: "first-error EPDs where loss ≥ inaccuracy"). It is not every mistake at ply ≤ 20.
  - Deviations with loss ≥ 5 are covered when they are the first error.
  - Missed punishments are covered only when they are the first error (see Gaps).
  - The deep confirmation threshold is also 5, not the plan's 10.
- **Mistake tolerance is 3 win%** (the brief: "as in judgeRetry"), not the critic's < 5. Line cards follow the critic: only the repertoire move counts, and a sound other move is neutral.
- **The deep check is lazy and capped**, per the critic's correction. Only today's new mistake candidates are checked, never all ~40 up front.
- **"Due today" introduces the day's new line cards on the first count.** Home or the badge introduces them, never mistake checks. The count therefore equals what a session offers.
- **The answer endpoint is `POST /api/drills/answer` with the id in the body**, not `/drills/:id/answer`. The id holds the EPD ('/' and spaces), as in P7a's critic note.
- **Regeneration is lazy, by a stamp**, rather than a hook in each writer. That covers a backfill run by the CLI in another process. Sync and repertoire writes also trigger it directly.
- **No "Add this position to drills" in the review, and no `POST /api/drills` for a manual card.** A manual card needs a "manual" flag that survives regeneration. Mistake positions from the owner's games already become cards, and "Drill this" on fix cards covers the flagged moves. This is left for P9.
- **The session line cap and re-queue.** A wrong line move is revealed at once, and the owner must play the repertoire move before the run continues. That replaces the "re-queue 3 cards later" rule for lines; mistake positions do re-queue 3 items later.
- **Session order.** A mixed session alternates one line run and two positions; a tab restricts the kind. Line runs are grouped by colour.
- **New line cards are ordered by fix-list relevance only indirectly** (owner edits first, then weight, the games reaching the node, which puts the lines' first moves first). Mistake games raise nothing yet.

## Known gaps / notes for P9

- **Review "Add this position to drills" / `POST /api/drills`** (see Deviations). It needs a manual flag in the card content, and `mergeCards` must keep manual cards.
- **Missed punishments are not a separate card source.** They count only when they are the game's first error. `analysis.missedPunish` is available.
- **Coverage is thin.** 49 of 1,375 games are fully engine-checked, and the 91 first errors come from games whose early positions happen to be cached. After the owner's full backfill, expect a few hundred mistake cards; the 5/day cap and the weighting keep sessions short.
- **The pending pool.** The `drills:check` job checks at most (slots + 2) candidates per session start; rejected cards free their slot for the next start.
- **The line headline's name** is the card's name at a graded step, else the run's final name, so during auto-play it can name a deeper line than the moves shown.
- **The weights** (8× target boost, 6 runs, 20 positions per session) are untuned.
- **Timezone.** The daily cap and "due today" use the server's local midnight; `nextDue` is an absolute time.
- **State survives restarts** because everything is in SQLite; the browser session itself does not (a reload starts a new session, and graded answers are kept).
