## What was done

### Review endpoint (`server/services/review.ts`, `shared/review.ts`)
- **`buildOpeningReview`** is pure. It takes a game's stored plies, an `EvalLookup`, the book and the config, and returns the `OpeningReview` DTO. It is built on `analyzeGameOpening` (P5).
  - Per ply:
    - the SAN, colour, owner flag, FEN before and after, and `inBook`;
    - `evalAfter`: White's side, the played move's score at its root;
    - `loss` and `cls`: exact for the owner; approximate for the opponent, flagged `approx`;
    - `lines`: the root's MultiPV lines, in SAN, from White's side;
    - `played`: the played move's own line, in SAN.
  - For the whole game:
    - `start.lines`: the eval before move 1;
    - `firstOwnerError` (a ply number, `null`, or `"pending"`);
    - `landing`: the first mistake, else the first move out of book;
    - `bookExit`: `lastBookPly`, `exitBy` and the deepest book `name`/`eco` reached;
    - `later`: the stored plies 21-30, shown greyed;
    - `totalPlies`, `status` and `coverage`.
- **`toReviewLine`** converts a UCI PV to SAN with chess.js and stops at the first move that does not apply. It moved here from `reviewMoves.ts`.
- **`reviewFromStore(db, id, configId | null, book)`**:
  - builds the review with about 20 `getPositionEval` lookups, so it does not need the in-memory analysis index;
  - writes the `game_analysis` row when the review is complete and the row is missing (P4b behaviour).
- **`completeReview`** runs `evaluateOpening` over the game's positions at interactive priority. The cache answers what it can, and only the missing moves or positions reach the pool.
- **`GET /api/games/:id/analysis`** returns `{review, job, engineError}`.
  - **200** when the review is complete, or when no engine config resolves (everything pending, with `engineError`).
  - Otherwise **202**, with the partial review and the `review:<id>` job from `startOrReuse`: two opens join one job. The job's result is the complete review.
  - 404 for a game that is not stored.
- **`POST /api/games/:id/retry {ply, uci}`** calls `retryMove`:
  - it validates the ply (it must be the owner's) and the legality of the move (400 otherwise);
  - it judges the move against the owner-tier row's best line with `judgeRetry`;
  - a move the row has not scored goes through `evaluateOpening` with `played: [uci]`, which means one depth-matched `searchmoves` follow-up on the cached row. The result is stored, so a second try is instant (`searched: false`).
- **Retry maths** (`shared/review.ts`): `retryVerdict(loss)` gives `correct` below 1 win%, `playable` below 3 and `try-again` otherwise. `RETRY_REVEAL_AFTER = 2`.
- **Removed**:
  - `POST /api/game-review`;
  - `server/services/reviewAnalysis.ts`, `reviewMoves.ts` (and its test) and `shared/notes.ts`;
  - `ReviewSummary`, `AnnotatedMove` and `ReviewGameHeader`, plus the workspace's in-memory review cache and review jobs.
  - `ReviewLine` is kept.

### Reusable board components (`src/components/`)
- **`EvalBar`**:
  - It is vertical, and White's share is White's win%, so it is above half whenever White is better.
  - White's end faces White's side of the board: the bottom when oriented to White, the top when oriented to Black, so it flips with the board.
  - The label (`+0.8`, `M3`, `-M3`) sits at the leading side's end.
  - It has `role="img"` with an aria-label.
  - Below 760 px it becomes a 14 px strip above the board, with White's end on the left when the board is oriented to White.
- **`ReviewBoard`**: a read-only board with arrows and highlights.
- **`PlayableBoard`**, for P8 to reuse:
  - it wraps react-chessboard 4: `onPieceDrop` returns a boolean, and `onSquareClick` gives tap-to-move with the picked square and its legal targets highlighted;
  - chess.js checks legality inside a try/catch, and `autoPromoteToQueen` is on;
  - `onMove(move) => boolean | void` lets the parent refuse a move;
  - a new `fen` or a new React `key` resets the board.
- **`MoveList`**:
  - SAN pairs. The owner's moves are coloured by class, with a class dot and `?!`, `?` or `??`. The opponent's moves are muted, and only an error (approximate loss ≥ 10) gets a `?`.
  - Book tags, and the out-of-book divider after the move pair that holds the last book ply.
  - The greyed later moves and a "Full game on Chess.com" link.
  - `aria-current="step"` on the selected move.
- **`PvLine`**: clickable SAN chips with move numbers and `aria-pressed`. A chip shows the board after that move of the line.

### Review page (`src/pages/GameReviewPage.tsx`, `src/styles/review.css`)
- **Header**: from `GET /api/games/:id`, as before.
- **Loading**: the page loads the analysis and polls the job with `useJobPolling` while it runs ("Checking the opening", with progress). It shows the review once it is complete, or once no job runs any more (a failed job or no engine gives a partial review with a note).
- **Landing**: `?ply=` (clamped to the window), else the first owner mistake, else the book exit. `?mode=retry` is kept.
- **Clean game**: shows "Clean opening: no mistakes in the first 10 moves".
- **Callouts**:
  - a mistake: "2...Bc5 is a mistake (−12% win chance)" / "Best was 2...Nc6";
  - the owner's best move: "Engine agrees: 3...Qa5 is the best move here". This copy appears only on the owner's moves;
  - an opponent's move: "4.Qf3 by your opponent" / "The engine preferred 4.Nf3.", or an "Opponent error" note marked approximate;
  - Best mode also lists the other engine moves.
- **Engine lines**: a "Best line" and "Your move's line" (or "The game's line"), as PvLine chips you can step through.
- **Retry**:
  - `PlayableBoard` at the position before the owner's move, oriented to the owner.
  - After a drop or tap it shows "Checking your move with Stockfish…" with a spinner until the verdict arrives.
  - **Correct**: "Correct: 2...Nc6 is as good as the engine's best".
  - **Good enough**: shown with its loss.
  - **Try again**: "2...Bc5 loses 12% win chance. Try again". The piece goes back after 0.9 s.
  - The best move (arrow and text) shows after a correct or good-enough move, after 2 misses, or on "Show the answer". "Retry again" resets the board.
  - The Retry chip is disabled on the opponent's plies.
- **Keyboard** (a window listener that ignores form fields and modifier keys):
  - ←/→ step through the moves, or through the engine line being shown; Home/End likewise;
  - ↑/↓ jump to the owner's previous or next mistake (or blunder);
  - `s`/`b`/`r` switch between Show, Best and Retry; `f` flips the board;
  - Esc leaves the line or Retry.
- **Accessibility**: every icon-only button has an aria-label: the previous move, the flip (with `aria-pressed`) and the next move. The mode chips carry `aria-pressed`.
- **Kept**: `calculateReviewBoardSize` and the mobile rules. The size now leaves 32 px for the bar at 760 px and above, and never drops below 160 px (react-chessboard drew negative SVG sizes during a viewport resize).
- **Removed**: the rail, `reviewCopy` and `buildModeState`, and the old eval-bar and rail CSS. The class-dot styles moved from `explorer.css` to the global sheet.

## Deviations from the spec, and why

- **No deep tier.** The spec wanted the owner's mistake positions searched at MultiPV 4 and 3M nodes. The brief makes this optional and lazy. Adding it to `ENGINE_PROTOCOL.tiers` would change `protocol_json`, and so create a new engine config and re-queue every game (the INCREMENTAL RULE). It would need its own tier row keyed outside the protocol, which P7 and P8 can add when they need it. The review never waits on it.
- **No JSONL deep cache.** The SQLite override applies, and there is no deep tier (above).
- **Retry verdicts**: Correct (< 1), "Good enough" (the spec's "Playable", < 3) and Try again (≥ 3), following the brief's wording. The answer shows after 2 misses (spec) and also after a success or on request.
- **"r/b/f change mode"**: the modes are `s`/`b`/`r` and `f` flips the board. The spec's "f" was ambiguous, and the brief orients the board to the owner (it resets to the owner for each game).
- **The review is judged on the server.** `POST /retry` is the one path, cached or not. A cached move answers in milliseconds, and an unknown one takes one follow-up search.
- **The first request after a server start takes about 1.1 s**: the engine id detection and the opening book build, both once per process. After that a backfilled review answers in **~70 ms**, and `verify-evals` times the cached review at **68 ms** (it had counted the book build before this phase).
- **The book divider sits after the move pair** that holds the last book ply, not between the two half-moves.
- **The opponent's moves show no class colour**, only an error mark, as in P5's Explorer (their search is approximate).
- **The workspace review cache is gone.** The page refetches the analysis each time, which is instant from the cache, so the browser keeps no copy.

## Known gaps / notes for P7 and later

- **Deep tier**: not built (see the deviations). If P7 or P8 want MultiPV 4 at 3M nodes for mistake positions, store them as a separate tier whose spec lives outside `ENGINE_PROTOCOL`, or as a new protocol version on purpose. Keep `TIER_ORDER`/`getPositionEval` and the analysis index's lookup from treating that tier as the owner tier by accident.
- **`PlayableBoard` for P8**:
  - it takes `fen`, `orientation`, `width`, `disabled`, `onMove` (return false to refuse), `arrows` and `squareStyles`;
  - re-key it to reset to the same position;
  - it allows several moves in a row: a drill can pass the reply's FEN, or keep `disabled` between moves.
- **Retry judging for drills**: `shared/review.ts` has `judgeRetry` and `retryVerdict`, and `POST /api/games/:id/retry` shows the on-demand flow. A drill endpoint that works on EPDs rather than games can reuse `evaluateOpening` with `played: [uci]` the same way.
- **The DTO for P7's alternatives panel**: the owner's plies carry the root's top-3 `lines` in SAN.
- `missedPunish` and `spentSec` from `analyzeGameOpening` are still not shown in the review.
- The review page scrolls the stage to the top on landing; on phones the sticky top bar covers the eval strip until you scroll a little (as before P6).
- **Run the full backfill (on mains)**: every review of an unchecked game runs its own small job (about 4-10 s on battery).
