## What was done

### Per-game opening analysis (`shared/openingAnalysis.ts`, computed, never stored)
- `analyzeGameOpening(game, lookup, book?, maxPly?)` takes a `TreeGame` (its plies are already loaded for the tree) and an `EvalLookup` (the cache: epd + tier → `PositionEval`, the best tier present). It returns `GameOpeningAnalysis`:
  - `coverage {ownerMoves, scored, plies, pliesScored}` and `status` (`complete` when every ply of the window is scored, else `partial`);
  - `evalWhite[i]`: the eval after ply i+1 from White's side (`{cp, mate, whiteWinPct}`; the played move's score at its root, as P4b's summary does), or `"pending"`;
  - `ownerMoves[]`: `{status: "scored", ply, san, uci, epdBefore, bestUci, bestSan, winBest, winPlayed, loss, cls, inBook}` or `{status: "pending", ...}`;
  - `firstOwnerError` (mistake or worse; `null` = clean, `"pending"` when an unscored owner move comes first), `firstInaccuracyPly` likewise;
  - `opponentErrors` (approxLoss ≥ 10, `approx: true`), `missedPunish` (after an opponent error, the owner's reply loses ≥ 5 while winBest ≥ 60);
  - `evalAt` for plies 10/16/20 (owner view, or pending), `bookExit` (the existing `bookExit` helper), `spentSec` over the first 10 own moves.
- Aggregates: `errorKnownThrough`, `firstErrorShares`, `topFirstMistakes`, `meanEvalAt`, and the coverage gate `coverageOk` (`ENGINE_MIN_GAMES` 5 and `ENGINE_MIN_COVERAGE` 0.5). A first-error rate over a span only counts games whose owner moves in the whole span are scored, so a game with an early error is not favoured over a clean game that is still partly pending.
- Every threshold is an exported constant.

### Analysis index (`server/services/analysisIndex.ts`)
- One in-memory map of a config's `positions` rows. The generation stamp is `COUNT(*)` + `MAX(analyzed_at)` (`positionsStamp`); on a change only rows with `analyzed_at >=` the last load are read (`listPositionEvals`), so polling during a backfill stays cheap.
- `analysisOf(game)` is memoised by game id and colour and cleared when the generation changes.
- The first tree request after start takes about 1 s (engine id detection, loading ~650 rows, the book); later ones take 20-30 ms.

### Tree enrichment (`shared/treeEngine.ts`, `shared/openingTree.ts`, `GET /api/tree`)
- `TreeEdgeView.engine`: `{status: "pending"}` or `{status: "scored", eval (after the move, White's side), loss, cls, winPlayed, isEngineBest (loss < 1), approx (opponent moves: MultiPV 1 at depth 14)}`.
- `TreeNodeView.engine`: the position's eval (White's side), `bestUci/bestSan`, `winBest`, `games` through the node and `complete`, the **hotspot** (owner's next 3 moves: known, errors, rate, `shown`, the top first mistakes with the engine's move), and `evalAt20` (mean owner eval after move 10, with `shown`).
- `TreeResponse.engine = {configId, games, complete}` gives "engine data for X of Y games". It is `null` when no engine config resolves (no Stockfish), and then every row's `engine` is `null`.
- Engine fields are computed per request for the one node (the games through it are found by EPD in the tree's games), so the memoised trees are unchanged.

### Fix list v1 (`shared/fixList.ts`, `GET /api/fixlist`)
- `FixItem = ResultsLeakItem | EngineHoleItem`. The old item is `ResultsLeakItem` plus `impact` (= `pointsLost`) and `engine: LeakEngineStats | null`.
- **`EngineHoleItem`** (`kind: "engine-hole"`, id `hole:<color>:<ucis>`): an owner edge with raw n ≥ 3 whose root eval scores it, gated per the critic: **loss ≥ 7**, or **loss ≥ 5 with the opponent's best reply ≥ +100 cp for him** (the reply from the child position's search, or the played move's score at the root when the child is not analysed). No results gate and not part of the BH family. It carries the loss and class, the best move, owner-view and White-view evals of the best and the played move, the reply, the gates it passed, `impact = wN × loss / 100`, the raw results, and examples.
- `LeakEngineStats` on each leak and watch item: games, complete, `evalAt20`, `firstError` (by ply 20) and `topFirstMistake`, each with `shown`.
- `buildFixList(trees, engine?)` returns the v0 selection plus `holes` and `ranked` (leaks and holes merged by impact). The response's `items` is the ranked list; it adds `holes`, `engine` per colour (coverage and the first-error shares by ply 10/16/20), `cap` (10) and the hole and engine thresholds. The memo also keys on the cache generation.

### Targeted line check (`server/services/lineAnalysis.ts`)
- `analyseLine` evaluates every position along a UCI line and the position it reaches. Each position is scored with every move the colour's window games played there, through `evaluateOpening` (cache first, results stored). It writes no `game_analysis` row.
- CLI: `npm run backfill -- --line e2e4,e7e5,g1f3,f8c5 --color black`.

## Deviations from the spec, and why

- **Hole gate = the critic's**, not "cls ≥ mistake". The reply arm also requires loss ≥ 5 (an inaccuracy): read literally, "the opponent's best reply ≥ +100 cp" would flag even the best move in a position that was already bad. So the arm is "an inaccuracy or worse that leaves the opponent ≥ +100". The acceptance is the critic's: the EPD after 2...Bc5 has loss ≥ 8 and best in {Nc6, Nf6, d6}. The "80% of 16 games" criterion is replaced, since all 16 share one eval; verify checks all 16 anyway.
- **Hotspot span = the owner's next 3 moves** (up to 6 plies), not "3 plies". It matches the UI copy "within 3 moves" (`HOTSPOT_OWNER_MOVES`).
- **The leak cards follow the brief** (average eval at move 10, the first-mistake rate by move 10, the most common first mistake with the engine's move), not the plan's median eval at ply 20 and first error by ply 16. **`spentFrac` against a baseline is not built.** `spentSec` is in the analysis, but no card uses it yet.
- **Home**: the leaks card gets the first-mistake shares by ply 10/16/20 per colour. The per-colour score with its CI was not added: the snapshot card already shows scores per answer.
- **`analyzeGameOpening` takes a `TreeGame`**, not a `GameRecord` (the plies live there). The tree's engine code is in the new `shared/treeEngine.ts`; `openingTree.ts` only gained the `nodeView` hook.
- **Engine fields are computed per request for one node**, not stored on the memoised tree.
- **Opponent edges also carry loss and class**, flagged `approx`. The UI shows them as a plain number with no class dot.
- **Scandinavian**: "does not flag the Scandinavian" is checked as "1...d5 itself is never flagged" (and no leak line starts 1.e4 d5, as before). A deeper engine hole in its 2.e5 sideline (3...Bf5, 11.1 win%) is listed, because it is exactly what the engine-hole kind exists for.
- **The theory-hole card shows evals from the owner's side** ("Eval for you −0.36 vs −1.71", the critic's "−0.4 vs −1.6" copy); the reply is given for the opponent ("+1.87 for White"). The Explorer is White's side throughout.
- **`verify-analysis` can write**: it stores the evals of the two targeted lines when they are missing (as the brief asks). Every other verify script stays read-only.
- **vitest `testTimeout` raised to 20 s.** With one more fake-engine route test, a 1.3 s backfill test took over 5 s in the parallel run on battery. It passed alone and failed in the full run.

## Known gaps / notes for later phases

- **Run the full backfill (on mains)** before judging the numbers: every hotspot, mistake rate and move-10 eval is hidden today, and the first-mistake shares cover only 25-70 known games.
- **Hole thresholds sit near real moves**: 2...Bc5 at 11.9 (d15, this run) against 9.5 in P4a's smoke with a cold hash. Holes with loss 7-9 (2...Bf5 after 1.g3, the Englund) can move with hash state. The constants are in `shared/fixList.ts`.
- **A hole's reply is missing when the child position is not cached** (1.c4 d5 2.Nc3, 1.d4 e5). The reply gate then uses the played move's root score; the backfill fills these in.
- The hotspot uses the games' own plies after the node, so a transposition counts its games from their own ply, while `fromPly/toPly` display the node's shortest ply.
- P6 (opening review) can reuse `analyzeGameOpening` directly: `missedPunish`, `opponentErrors`, `bookExit` and `spentSec` are computed but not shown yet.
- P7 (repertoire/alternatives) can use `EngineHoleItem.bestSan` and the tree's `node.engine.bestUci` for "the engine's move" suggestions.
- `tsc --noUnusedLocals` still reports only the two P3 leftovers in `routes.ts`.
